FAQ

Good questions.

How is pricing calculated?

It depends on the type of project, how complex it is, the time required and materials. Many smaller jobs get a flat rate, and larger or mixed projects may be hourly. Minimum service charge depends on location and scope.

Can you do several jobs in one visit?

Yes, and I recommend it. One recent visit covered furniture assembly, a TV mount, shelves and smart-home setup, done in about 4 hours including testing and clean-up.

What should I send for a quote?

Photos, a short description, model numbers and measurements if you have them. Also mention anything special: stairs, high ceilings, concrete or brick walls, or limited access. It helps me bring the right tools and give an accurate price.

Do I need to buy the devices or parts?

Usually you have the item (TV, doorbell, thermostat, furniture). If you're not sure what to buy for your home, I can recommend compatible smart-home devices before you order.

What areas do you serve?

I'm based in Lake Nona and serve Orlando and nearby areas. Send your ZIP code and I'll confirm.

Do you speak Spanish or Portuguese?

Sí. Sim. I work in English, Spanish and Portuguese.
